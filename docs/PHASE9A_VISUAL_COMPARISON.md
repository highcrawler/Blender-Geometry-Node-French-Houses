Visual inspection: the alternating seams and horizontal courses remain recognizable on both native 480×360 frames; the cottage occupies roughly 300 vertical pixels. AGED remains identifiable as the same shingle covering; moss is faint and local rather than a competing roof material. Close-ups reveal regular straight course lines and conspicuously uniform flat, grain-free material; the hand-cut edge variation is subtle. Front/gable view exposes the thin deck/ridge and does not reveal a thatch-like silhouette. The selected ridge/eave oblique framing favors the eave; it does not isolate a magnified ridge end. No texture mapping faults were seen because the materials do not use textures/UV. This is an art-direction limitation rather than evidence of missing geometry.

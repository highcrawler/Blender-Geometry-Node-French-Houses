# Phase 9C — geometry, regression and visual validation

[Six-case iso overview](../experiments/wooden_roof_system_09c/overview_six_iso.jpg) · [Six front/gable views](../experiments/wooden_roof_system_09c/overview_six_front.jpg) · [Same-camera comparison with Long and Compact](../experiments/wooden_roof_system_09c/comparison_with_references.jpg) · [T03/T05 ridge and eave details](../experiments/wooden_roof_system_09c/failure_details.jpg). All 26 source images in [screenshots](../experiments/wooden_roof_system_09c/screenshots) were rendered by Blender 5.2.2 Cycles with 12 samples, denoising, identical orthographic camera and two-area-light rig for every iso; all front views share another fixed camera. The views are not beauty re-framed per house. Close-ups use the tested roof's own ridge/eave target. The steep roof initially touched a frame edge; the final shared camera was repositioned for **all eight** isometric cases, and all renders were regenerated.

The [reusable 9B audit](../experiments/wooden_roof_system_09b/audit.py) checks every closed shingle (eight vertices, six quads, two faces incident to each edge, positive signed volume), nonzero triangulated face area, unique coordinates, each deck and ridge solid, actual Gable course coverage and gaps, non-final course overlap, final course reaching the ridge and minimum shingle length >0.10 m. It reports physical coordinates, lengths, per-course counts, and gaps. Overlap of neighboring courses is intentional; the audit does not claim a self-intersection-free fused solid. On the exploratory inputs its assertions are recorded rather than raised, without changing the source thresholds. The maximum *measured* gaps are 0.08324 m at the gable and 0.02366 m internally, both under the original <0.09/<0.04 m limits. No mesh topology/volume/area/duplicate or gap-bound failure was observed. T06's shortest final shingle is 0.10728 m, only 7.28 mm above the previously documented 0.10 m technical floor: this is a near-limit result, not a proof that steeper/other inputs work.

**Actual defect:** 9A computes `n_courses=ceil(slope_length/0.185)`, but the row loop skips a final row when its start lies within 0.06 m of the ridge. For T03 `courses` reports 22 although 21 rows per slope exist; T05 reports 19 although 18 exist. This is a metadata/count inconsistency (the 9B strict audit would raise on missing row), not an exposed ridge: each final *existing* course still ends 0.025 m past the center under the ridge planks. The 9C recorder reports both numbers and marks these cases FAIL. It does **not** change the 9A generator, create a compensating row or raise the gap thresholds. Both failure-region close-ups show the same simple ridge plank, a narrow dark eave shadow already present in the reference and no new visible roof opening. Visual assessment of the six roofs: **ALL PASS**, although two metadata checks FAIL. Project-lead artistic approval is separate.

The roof and nonroof mesh fingerprints of CLEAN and AGED match within **every** case. Each case/state is regenerated with the same seed and the hashes match. Long CLEAN matches the saved 9A `.blend` roof and body; Compact CLEAN matches the saved 9B `.blend` after reversing its saved +11 m display offset. Their 9B measurements also match. Both references are rebuilt *after* the six cases and produce the same hashes, ruling out inter-case RNG bleed in this fixed matrix. These tests do not validate the full continuous parameter space, material texture mapping, FPS, Godot export or complex roof intersections.
